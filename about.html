About Carolina BuySide

A student platform for learning how investors actually think.

Founded in 2025, Carolina BuySide is built for students who want to understand private markets through structured learning, practical discussion, and a career-facing community. As a buy-side club we explicitly span private equity, growth equity, and venture capital — and we emphasize public-to-private pitches, sector work, and a more disciplined approach to investment thinking.

Why We Exist

Two things the club is trying to do.

Everything the organization runs — onboarding, sector teams, pitch nights, speaker events — ladders up to these.

Mission

Build investing judgment before the stakes are real.

The club sits at the intersection of education, professional development, and peer accountability. Members learn the language and frameworks of private markets while getting repeated practice articulating what they believe and why.

Community

Create a clearer path into PE, VC, and related roles.

Carolina BuySide gives students a shared environment to ask better questions, sharpen their analysis, and learn from more experienced peers, alumni, and industry guests across PE, GE, and VC-focused paths.

Programming

What members can expect from the club.

One buy-side foundation

Rather than splitting into separate tracks, members build a single foundation across private equity, growth equity, and venture capital — shared diligence frameworks, risk judgment, and decision-making practice that transfer between them.

Research you present

Work does not stop at a written summary. Members evaluate businesses and sectors together, then put a public-to-private pitch in front of the group and defend it.

Access to people doing the work

Programming brings in founders, investors, and Carolina alumni, connecting what members are learning to real recruiting timelines and career paths.

Partnership

Club partner: Perplexity.

P

Modern research tooling, taught deliberately.

Carolina BuySide is a club partner with Perplexity. The partnership reflects how the club approaches research — using modern tools to move faster on information gathering, while keeping the analytical judgment that turns information into an actual investment view.

AI Utility

Teaching members to use AI effectively in modern research.

Most investing clubs either ban AI or ignore it. We treat it as a research skill worth teaching properly — speed where it helps, scepticism where it matters.

AI as a research accelerator

Carolina BuySide emphasizes practical AI utility as part of the research process. Members learn how to use AI tools to accelerate information gathering, sharpen question framing, and structure first-pass diligence more efficiently.

Judgment still comes first

The club also teaches responsible use: verifying outputs, pressure-testing assumptions, and separating tool-assisted speed from true investment judgment. The goal is better analysts, not just faster prompts.

How We Operate

Three things we hold members to.

01

Have a view

Summarizing consensus is not analysis. Members are expected to land somewhere and say why, even when the answer is that a business is not worth owning.

02

Show your work

A thesis is only as good as the reasoning under it. Assumptions get stated out loud so they can be challenged rather than quietly carried.

03

Compound over semesters

Sector coverage carries forward. Members build on industries they already know instead of restarting from zero on every new name.
