Questions

Before you apply

Do I need prior finance experience?
No. Onboarding starts from private markets fundamentals, so no coursework or internship background is assumed. What matters is that you are willing to do the research, form a view, and defend it when someone pushes back.
What majors and class years can apply?
All majors and all class years. Members come in with different backgrounds and the sector structure is designed to let people start wherever they are.
Is there a resume drop or a coffee chat requirement?
Neither. There is one application form and one deadline. You do not need to have spoken to anyone in the club beforehand, and reaching out early is not scored.
What happens after I submit?
The form closes end of day Sunday, September 6. Applications are reviewed after the deadline, selected applicants are contacted by email, and every applicant is told their outcome once decisions are made.
What happens if I’m selected?
We will reach out by email to request some additional information. Keep an eye on the inbox you applied with, and check your spam folder if you have not heard anything once decisions go out.
Do I pick a sector when I join?
Yes. New members choose one of our six coverage areas during onboarding. Prior knowledge of that industry is not required — a reason you want to follow it is.
How do I get updates before then?
Recruiting announcements go out through LinkedIn. For anything not answered here, email carolinabuyside@gmail.com and we will get back to you.
