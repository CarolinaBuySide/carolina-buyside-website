Sector Coverage

Members don't cover everything. They cover something.

Sector teams are how Carolina BuySide turns general interest into real familiarity. Members follow an industry over time, compare businesses within it, and translate that research into public-to-private pitches relevant to PE, GE, and VC decision-making.

How It Works

Follow, analyze, pitch.

Each sector team runs the same loop every semester. Repetition is the point — judgment comes from doing this more than once.

Follow an industry

Members track companies, themes, and news within their sector so they build context that compounds instead of starting cold on every new name.

Analyze businesses

Teams compare business models, unit economics, and competitive position — developing a view on what actually makes a company worth owning.

Pitch and defend

Research becomes a public-to-private pitch presented to the group, where assumptions get pressure-tested and the thesis has to hold up out loud.

The Deliverable

What a pitch actually contains.

Sector work builds toward one thing: a defensible recommendation. Every pitch is expected to answer these five questions, in this order.

01

Thesis

What you believe about this business, stated in a sentence, and where that differs from how the market currently sees it.

02

Business quality

How the company actually makes money — unit economics, competitive position, and what protects margins over time.

03

Valuation

What the business is worth under stated assumptions, what you would be willing to pay, and which inputs the answer is most sensitive to.

04

Risks

The specific things that would make the thesis wrong — not a generic risk list, but the two or three that would actually change the recommendation.

05

Recommendation

The call itself, and what you would watch after making it to know whether the thesis is playing out.
